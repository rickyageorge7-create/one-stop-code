Questions students ask

Do I need a laptop?

A laptop is best, but you can follow along on a phone with a code editor app to start.

Yes. The class starts from zero and every lesson builds on the one before.

What if my internet is slow?

Every session is recorded, and the notes are text-based, so they load fast on low data.

Is there a refund?

If you cancel before the first session, you get a full refund. After the first session there are no refunds, but you can give your seat to a friend or move to the next class.
